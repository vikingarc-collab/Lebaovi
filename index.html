About Vikcom

A trusted operations partner for global e-commerce businesses

Vikcom is a consulting company specializing in operations solutions for international e-commerce platforms. Our team brings more than 10 years of hands-on experience in the Print-on-Demand (POD) market – from niche research, design and store setup to advertising, fulfillment and customer service.

We have partnered with numerous businesses in the United States, helping them build stable operations, stay compliant with marketplace policies and achieve sustainable growth.

Our Mission

To help businesses operate on global e-commerce platforms professionally, efficiently and profitably.
